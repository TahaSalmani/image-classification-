Some people are at increased risk for pneumonia due to age, other health conditions, and certain behaviors.

Age:

- adults 65 years or older
- children younger than 5 years old

Risk rises with older age in adults and younger age in children.

Medical conditions:

- chronic heart disease
- chronic liver disease
- chronic lung disease
- diabetes
- a weakened immune system (this carries the greatest risk)

Lifestyle and environment:

- being around people who are sick
- drinking alcohol excessively
- smoking cigarettes

Season: fall and winter see more spread of respiratory germs, although pneumonia can occur at any time of the year.
