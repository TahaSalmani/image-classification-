Symptoms of pneumonia can start suddenly or gradually over a few days.

They can include:

- a cough
- shortness of breath
- a high temperature
- chest pain
- an aching body
- feeling very tired
- loss of appetite
- making wheezing noises when you breathe – babies may also make grunting noises
- feeling confused – this is common in older people
