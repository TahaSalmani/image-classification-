Symptoms of pneumonia can include:

- "Chest pain when breathing or coughing"
- "Altered mental status (confusion)"
- "Cough"
- "Fatigue (feeling tired)"
- "Fever or chills"
- "Nausea, vomiting, or diarrhea"
- "Shortness of breath"

Pneumonia is a lung infection. "Bacteria and viruses are common causes of pneumonia." "Fungi and parasites are less common causes."

Community and healthcare settings expose people to different germs, and "healthcare providers aren't always able to find out which germ caused someone to get sick with pneumonia."
