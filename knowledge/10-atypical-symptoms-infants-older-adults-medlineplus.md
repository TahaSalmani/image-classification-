"The symptoms of pneumonia can range from mild to severe."

Newborns and infants "may not show any signs of the infection."

Older adults and people with serious illness or weak immunity "may have fewer and milder symptoms". They "may even have a lower-than-normal temperature" and sometimes "feel weak or suddenly get confused."

Because symptoms can be mild or unusual in these groups, pneumonia in an infant or an older adult may not look like pneumonia at all.
