People can lower their risk of pneumonia with immunizations and other healthy living practices.

Vaccines that help protect against bacterial causes include Hib (Haemophilus influenzae type b), pertussis (whooping cough) and pneumococcal vaccines.

For viral causes, "Vaccines, antibody products, or both" may help prevent infection. This covers COVID-19, influenza (flu), measles, respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) and varicella (chickenpox).

Everyday prevention steps:

- "Avoid people who are sick" – and if you become ill, keep your distance from others
- "Washing your hands regularly"
- "Cleaning and disinfecting surfaces that are touched a lot"
- "Coughing or sneezing into a tissue or into your elbow or sleeve"
- "Limiting contact with cigarette smoke or quitting smoking"
- "Taking good care of chronic medical conditions (like asthma)"
